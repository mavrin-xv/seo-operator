## Method

| Step | What to do | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Grind | Coarse, like raw sugar | Fine grounds over-extract and turn bitter |
| Mix | 125 g coffee to 1 litre cold water | The 1:8 ratio |
| Steep | 18 hours in the fridge | Under 12 tastes thin, past 24 turns woody |
| Filter | Paper filter, then bottle | Removes the silt |

## FAQ

### Q: What ratio of coffee to water should I use for cold brew?

Use 1:8 by weight for coffee you drink straight. Go to 1:5 if you dilute it with milk or ice.

### Q: Why does my cold brew taste bitter?

The grind is too fine or it steeped too long. Change one of the two per batch.
