# How to Make Cold Brew at Home

Cold brew is coffee steeped in cold water for a long time instead of brewed hot. It tastes smoother and less acidic, and a batch keeps in the fridge for days.

## What ratio of coffee to water should I use?

Use 1 part coarse ground coffee to 8 parts cold water by weight. For one litre of water that is 125 grams of coffee. Go stronger (1:5) if you plan to dilute it with milk or ice. Most people find cold brew 3 times smoother than hot coffee.

## How long should cold brew steep?

Steep it for 18 hours in the fridge. Under 12 hours it tastes thin; past 24 hours it turns woody.

## Why does my cold brew taste bitter?

- The grind is too fine
- It steeped too long
- The beans are a dark roast

Cut the steep by 2 hours or grind coarser, and change one thing per batch.
